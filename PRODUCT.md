# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two readers, one portfolio:

- **Studios and recruiters**: scanning for published Unity mobile game experience, professionalism, and shipping track record. Need to feel confident within 30 seconds.
- **Indie developers and collaborators**: looking for a peer with taste, warmth, and real game credits. Need to feel like they found someone in the same tribe.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Serhii Chekun, Unity mobile game developer. Generates inbound interest: job offers, freelance inquiries, and creative collaborations. Success means a visitor who reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Warm, curious, indie. The small-studio developer who makes games because they love the medium, not to chase trends. Approachable enough for a recruiter, credible enough for a fellow developer.

## Anti-references

- Dark neon cyberpunk: cyan and pink glows on near-black (previous design direction)
- Generic developer portfolios: dark background, syntax-highlight accent colors, Orbitron-style display type
- SaaS landing pages masquerading as creative portfolios
- Editorial-magazine aesthetics: display serif, italic drops, broadsheet grid

## Design Principles

1. **Pixel soul, modern execution**: the design winks at game culture without being cosplay. The reference is felt, not quoted literally.
2. **Bright and alive**: light backgrounds, saturated color, no glows on dark. Color comes from a game's UI, not the screen's backlight.
3. **Warm first, professional second**: for this audience, warmth IS the professional signal. Cold minimalism reads as corporate.
4. **Show the work, trust the visitor**: the games are the hero. The design frames them without overpowering them.
5. **One portfolio, two readers**: recruiter at 9am and indie dev at midnight both feel this was made for them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. No special color-blindness requirements.
